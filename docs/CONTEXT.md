# Personal tutorial learning

A personal collection of tutorial lessons organized into courses. The learner finds lessons, watches them in sequence, and retains their place across watching sessions.

## Language

### Library organization

**Library**:
The learner's collection of courses and their associated learning material.
_Avoid_: Media server, account, workspace

**Course**:
A top-level tutorial collection containing lessons, optionally grouped into folders.
_Avoid_: Playlist, channel, category

**Lesson folder**:
A named group within a course that can contain lessons or further lesson folders.
_Avoid_: Course, playlist, mandatory module

**Lesson**:
One watchable instructional item, independent of how many files are needed to present it.
_Avoid_: File, rendition, conversion

**Source media**:
The original video and any companion audio from which a lesson is presented.
_Avoid_: Playback copy, cache

**Companion audio**:
A separate audio recording that belongs to a particular source video.
_Avoid_: Lesson, subtitle, soundtrack choice

### Watching

**Rendition**:
A playable representation of a lesson with a particular picture size and media format.
_Avoid_: Lesson, course

**Permanent playback copy**:
A retained compatible rendition prepared from source media.
_Avoid_: Original, disposable cache

**Quality version**:
An additional rendition prepared to offer a particular playback quality and eligible for later removal.
_Avoid_: Permanent playback copy, source

**Subtitle track**:
Timed text associated with a lesson that the learner can display or turn off.
_Avoid_: Notes, transcript search

**Viewing progress**:
The learner's remembered position and completion state for a lesson.
_Avoid_: Conversion progress, download progress

**Completion**:
The state indicating that the learner has finished a lesson, determined by watching or a manual choice.
_Avoid_: Playback position, prepared

**Continue learning**:
The entry point back into a course using the learner's most recent relevant viewing progress.
_Avoid_: Recently imported, conversion queue

### Preparation

**Preparation**:
The work needed to make a rendition available for watching.
_Avoid_: Download, import, scan

**Preparation job**:
A tracked request to prepare a particular rendition of a lesson.
_Avoid_: Lesson, viewing session

**Library scan**:
The discovery and reconciliation of courses, lessons, and associated material currently present in the library.
_Avoid_: Conversion, preparation

**Missing lesson**:
A previously known lesson whose source media cannot currently be located.
_Avoid_: Deleted progress, completed lesson
